ABOUT US

Exceptional spaces are never created all at once. They're built layer by layer. House of LAYR is a design-led destination for thoughtfully curated furniture, lighting, décor, and objects that bring character, balance, and purpose to every interior. We collaborate with architects, interior designers, developers, and discerning homeowners to create spaces that are timeless, functional, and deeply considered. Every collection is selected with a focus on craftsmanship, enduring quality, and refined aesthetics. Rather than following trends, we curate pieces that complement great architecture and elevate everyday living. At House of LAYR, we believe every detail contributes to the story of a space. Our role is to help complete that story with carefully chosen layers that leave a lasting impression.
