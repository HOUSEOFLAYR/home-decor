The Philosophy

Exceptional spaces are never created all at once. They're built layer by layer.

House of LAYR is a design-led destination for thoughtfully curated furniture, lighting, décor and objects that bring character, balance and purpose to every interior.

We collaborate with architects, interior designers, developers and discerning homeowners to help complete their vision, from the first idea to the final, considered detail.
